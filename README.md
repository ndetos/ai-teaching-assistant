## What Is This?

An AI tutor that runs on your laptop. Students connect via browser and ask questions. The AI answers using **YOUR course materials** — not generic internet content.

- **No internet required** during class
- **Students ask anonymously** — no fear, no embarrassment
- **Analytics dashboard** shows you what they didn't understand
- **Your data never leaves your laptop**
